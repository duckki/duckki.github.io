<p class="page-intro">Love Formal is Duckki Oe’s notebook about software engineering, programming languages, program analysis, and formal verification.</p>

I use this site to work through the design and behavior of software: language semantics, program analysis, formal models, verification techniques, and the engineering choices that connect specifications to implementations. Some investigations produce machine-checked proofs; others produce counterexamples, executable models, audits, or simply a sharper question. The essays favor concrete examples and explicit reasoning over broad surveys.

You can find my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/duckki), connect on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/duckki), subscribe through [RSS]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}), or [send me an email](mailto:{{ site.email }}).
